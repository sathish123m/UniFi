The Problem

Campus micro-cashflow gaps lack modern financial solutions.

⏳
Traditional Banks Fail Students
Standard banks require income proof, credit scores, collateral, and parents' signatures — taking weeks for approval.
💸
Unplanned Micro-Expenses
Students face unexpected costs (exam fees, lab materials, hostel rent, laptops) that cannot wait until monthly stipends arrive.
🛑
Informal Borrowing Stress
Borrowing from peers informally creates social friction, lacks clear repayment schedules, and leaves no digital credit footprint.

The Solution

Zero paperwork. 100% digital peer-to-peer campus marketplace.

⚡
Direct Student-to-Student Loans
Connects verified student borrowers with student providers looking for short-term, yield-generating investments.
  • Flexible tenures: 7, 14, 30, 60, or 90 days.
  • Micro-amounts: ₹500 to ₹50,000.
  • Instant UPI disbursal upon provider funding.
🎓
Verified University Ecosystem
Strict university email domain enforcement ensures only verified campus peers participate.
  • Institutional email validation (@lpu.in, @campus.edu).
  • Campus-only marketplace visibility.
  • Digital credit building for early-career students.
