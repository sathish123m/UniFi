Frequently asked

How is my credit limit decided?
Your limit is set by the campus admin based on your KYC status, verified profile and repayment history. It grows as you repay on time.
What happens if I miss a repayment?
You'll get reminders before every due date. Missed payments are flagged to the admin and may reduce your credit limit for future requests.
Can I repay before the due date?
Yes — you can make a full or partial repayment any time from the Repayment Center.
